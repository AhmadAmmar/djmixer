        Paste a list of titles, or open an M3U or CSV file. For a Spotify playlist, Exportify writes a CSV of its track names. Spotify does not let other apps read its audio, so each track still needs a file you own. Press Load beside a track to attach one.

          Keys

          Space play deck A. Enter play deck B. S sync B to A. Left and Right move the crossfader. C mark a cue on the deck being followed. 1 to 4 jump to a cue. Scroll over a waveform to zoom. Click a waveform to jump there.
